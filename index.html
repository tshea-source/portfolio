How I work

Four habits that show up in every launch.

01

Understand the product deeply.

Embed with the product team. Play with the product, navigate demo environments comfortably, and become the SME for the business side of the house.

02

Siphon out the value.

Find the real problem the offering solves and the specific value it delivers to customers. Strip the feature list until what's left is the story.

03

Empower the field.

Communicate value clearly to marketing and sales. Build the assets they need, then arm them to spread the word in their own voice.

04

Measure. Iterate. Repeat.

Analyze what's working in the sales process and in the funnel. Optimize the asset, the sequence, or the message — whichever is the bottleneck.
